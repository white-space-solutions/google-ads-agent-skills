# Ad copy for seller-direct campaigns

Read with `docs/playbook/ads-rsa-spec.md` (structure, pinning, limits) and `docs/playbook/ads-assets-spec.md`.

## Angles that work for sellers

| Angle | Headline examples (≤30 chars) |
|---|---|
| Keyword + place (pin slot 1) | Sell Your House Fast {City} · We Buy Houses In {City} · Cash Home Buyers {City} |
| Speed | Close In As Little As 7 Days · Pick Your Closing Date |
| Money certainty | Fair Cash Offer In 24 Hours · No Fees Or Commissions |
| Condition | Sell As-Is. No Repairs. · Skip The Cleaning And Showings |
| Situation | Inherited A House? We Can Help · Behind On Payments? Talk To Us |
| Trust (verified only) | Local Buyer Since {year} · {N}+ Houses Bought In {City} |
| CTA | Get Your Cash Offer Today · Call For A No-Obligation Offer |

Descriptions: one per angle (speed, money, condition, trust), 61 to 90 characters, ending in a clear next step.

## Proof rules

- Every number in an ad must be true and on the landing page: years in business, houses bought, review rating.
- "Fair" and "top dollar" are claims. Prefer specifics: "No fees, no commissions, we pay closing costs" only if you do.
- Do not promise a close date you cannot hit. "As little as 7 days" is fine when it has happened.

## Compliance

- No competitor names in ad text. Bid on them, do not write them.
- Avoid implying you are a government program, a lender, or foreclosure relief service. In many states foreclosure rescue language is regulated. Say "we buy houses in any situation", not "stop foreclosure".
- Do not imply you are the seller's agent. You are the buyer.

## Assets

- Sitelinks: How It Works, Get My Offer, Inherited Houses, Foreclosure Help, Reviews, About Us. Each to a real page.
- Callouts: No Fees, No Repairs, Close In 7 Days, Local Buyer, Cash Offer In 24 Hrs, Any Condition.
- Structured snippet "Types": Inherited, Rentals, Fixer-Uppers, Vacant, Probate, Divorce.
- Call asset: only if a human answers during ad hours. Missed calls waste the most expensive clicks in the account.
