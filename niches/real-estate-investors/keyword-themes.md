# Keyword themes for seller-direct campaigns

Use these as ad group themes. One theme per ad group, one landing page per theme, phrase and exact match. Add broad match only to a theme that already converts and only with the negative lists attached.

## Campaign: Sell House Fast (generic)

| Ad group | Example keywords | Landing page angle |
|---|---|---|
| Sell house fast | sell my house fast, sell house fast, need to sell my house fast, sell my house fast {city} | Speed: close in 7 to 30 days |
| Cash buyers | we buy houses, cash home buyers, companies that buy houses for cash, cash offer for my house | Fair cash offer, no fees |
| As is | sell house as is, sell house that needs repairs, sell fixer upper | No repairs, no cleaning |
| Without a realtor | sell house without realtor, sell house no commission, sell by owner | Keep the commission, skip showings |

## Campaign: Situations (generic)

| Ad group | Example keywords |
|---|---|
| Inherited and probate | sell inherited house, probate home buyers, sell house in probate |
| Foreclosure | sell house before foreclosure, avoid foreclosure sell house, behind on mortgage sell house |
| Landlords | sell rental property, sell house with tenants, tired landlord |
| Divorce | sell house during divorce, sell house after divorce |
| Tax and liens | sell house with tax lien, behind on property taxes sell house |
| Damage | sell fire damaged house, sell hoarder house, sell house with foundation problems |

Situation pages should speak to the situation in the first screen. A generic "we buy houses" page converts these searches worse.

## Campaign: Competitors

One ad group per competitor, exact and phrase on the name and its common misspellings. Do not use competitor names in ad text (trademark). Expect higher cost per lead; judge it on qualified leads, not form fills.

## Campaign: Branded

Own name, misspellings, "name reviews". Target impression share with a CPC cap. Cheap insurance against competitors bidding on your name.

## Campaign: Home Value (test)

"how much is my house worth", "what is my house worth {city}", "home value estimator". Separate budget, its own page with an instant value range and a cash offer step. Judge after 60 days on qualified leads.

## Geography

- Location option: **Presence** (people in or regularly in the area), never "presence or interest".
- Exclude the cities you do not buy in as locations, and keep them in the Out of Area negative list too: sellers often type a city name while located elsewhere.
- Add "{city}" variants of the top 3 themes for each core city you serve.
