# Benchmarks (2026, accounts we ran or audited)

Ranges, not targets. Markets differ: a high-cost coastal metro runs two to three times a mid-size inland market. Judge an account against its own trend and its cost per **qualified** lead, not form fills.

| Metric | Mid-size market | High-cost metro | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPC, generic seller intent | $25 to $45 | $45 to $90 | "sell my house fast {city}" is usually the top of the range |
| CPC, competitor names | $20 to $60 | $40 to $100 | |
| Cost per lead (form or call) | $120 to $300 | $250 to $600 | |
| Cost per qualified lead (CRM-confirmed) | $200 to $450 | $400 to $1,100 | Performance Max often lowest in-account; budget-capped search highest |
| Lead to qualified | 25% to 45% | 20% to 40% | |
| Qualified to contract | 5% to 12% | 4% to 10% | |
| Branded CPC | $3 to $25 | $5 to $30 | Higher usually means competitors bid on your name |

Signals that something is wrong:

- Cost per qualified lead doubles over a quarter while spend holds. Check conversion value changes and upload health before touching bids.
- A search campaign losing more than half its impression share to budget at a high CPC. It cannot learn; fund it or fold it.
- More than a third of search term spend on zero-conversion terms.
- Mobile at 75 to 85% of spend is normal for sellers. Judge mobile on cost per qualified lead, not conversion rate.
