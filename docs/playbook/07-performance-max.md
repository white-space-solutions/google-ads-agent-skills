# 07 Performance Max

Performance Max often produces the cheapest leads in an account and is the hardest to read. It shows on Search, Maps, YouTube, Display, Gmail and Discover from one budget, and it optimizes toward your conversion goals with little visibility.

## When it works

- Strong, stable conversion signal (qualified leads uploaded from the CRM, stable values). PMax amplifies whatever you feed it.
- Enough budget to learn (at least 3 to 5x the target cost per conversion per day is a common floor).
- Brand exclusions set, so it does not take credit for brand searches.

## How to control it

| Lever | How |
|---|---|
| What it optimizes | Campaign conversion goals. Bid on qualified lead, not form fill. |
| Where it spends | Search term categories (`pmax_search_categories.json` from pull.py) and the Insights tab. |
| What it must not show for | Account-level negative keyword list for the worst offenders; brand exclusions. |
| Overlap with Search | Exact-match Search keywords win over PMax for the same query. Keep your core themes in Search to keep control. |
| Creative | Real photos and videos of the business beat stock. Upload a short video; otherwise Google auto-generates one. |

## Red flags

- Cost per qualified lead climbs while spend holds: check conversion values and uploads first (chapter 02).
- Most conversions are low-value secondary actions: fix the goals.
- Spend shifts to Display or YouTube placements with no qualified leads: add placement exclusions and check the asset group's audience signals.
