# Example Home Buyers: Google Ads audit
Fall 2026 · account 000-000-0000

## Summary

Qualified leads fell by about a third from summer to fall while spend held steady, and cost per qualified lead roughly doubled in both non-brand Search campaigns. The account is well built in places (custom bidding goals, CRM uploads, clean negative routing), so the fixes are about signal stability and where the budget goes, not a rebuild.

Top fixes, ranked by expected impact:

1. **Lock the conversion value model.** Qualified Lead value roughly quintupled in ten weeks. Performance Max bids on value, so its target keeps moving.
2. **Give each conversion action one upload owner.** Three systems write to the same actions; one backfill had hundreds of failed rows.
3. **Rebuild Competitors off its single broad keyword.** It drifts into listings, rentals and Zillow searches.
4. **Fund or fold the starved search campaigns.** Two campaigns lose about 90% of impression share to budget at around $50 a click.
5. **Fix negatives that block real sellers.** "mortgage" and "realtor" block pre-foreclosure and FSBO searches in Performance Max.
6. **Test a valuation campaign.** "How much is my house worth" searches produced about 10 qualified leads for under $500 over the year, and no campaign owns them.

Nothing in the account was changed for this audit.
